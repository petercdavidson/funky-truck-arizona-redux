Tile sheet sets

Each set is tinted its own colour. A red line between two tiles: they don't join. No line: they do (the art carries on from one to the other).

Click the edge between two tiles to join or part them. Drag a box to join every tile inside it to its neighbours inside it; Alt-drag to part them all.

yellow numbers: ground pieces
cyan numbers: art
grey: empty
