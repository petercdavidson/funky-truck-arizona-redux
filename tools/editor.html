Paint: left drag paints the selected tile, right drag erases to sky. Alt-click picks up the tile under the cursor. Shift-click sets where the truck drops in. Select (S): drag a box, Ctrl+C copies, Ctrl+V then click to stamp it (Esc stops). Del clears the box. Shift moves the whole level (Shift+arrow keys: 1 tile). Path (D): click to add points to a curve, and the editor lays the tiles that follow it (with the fill below), joining the ground on either side. Drag a point or its handles to reshape it (Alt-drag a handle to bend at the point); right-click a point to remove it, double-click to make it smooth again. The tiles update as you go; New path keeps them and starts another curve. Tiles are 64 px; 1 tile is 2.5 m.
