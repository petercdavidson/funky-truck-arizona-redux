Stage Editor: keyboard and mouse

Tools

B PaintDrag paints the chosen tile, right-drag erases (E picks sky to paint: erase). Picking a tile from the tiles switches to Paint. Alt-click picks up a tile. H mirrors the chosen tile, V turns it upside down, R turns it a quarter (Shift+R the other way).
S SelectDrag a box; drag inside to move it, Alt-drag to copy. H flips it left to right, V upside down, R turns it; arrows nudge it, ⌘D duplicates it (the copy floats over it: drag it away), Del clears it, Enter drops it, Esc lets go.
G PathClick to add points to a curve; the tiles follow it, the ground on the side with the brown ticks (any way round: walls, overhangs, ceilings). Drag points and handles; Alt-drag a handle for a corner, Alt-click the point to line its handles up again. Right-click removes a point; right-click elsewhere or Esc finishes the path.
Q ShapeDrag a box for a circle, square or diamond of ground (ground out), or a hole that shape (ground in). Shift keeps it round.
T StartClick where the truck drops in (or Shift-click while painting).
F FinishClick where the finish line goes.

Everywhere

Pinch, ⌘/Ctrl+scrollZoom at the pointer. + − zoom, 0 fits the height.
Scroll, Space+drag, middle-dragMove around. The strip at the bottom shows the whole level: click it to jump.
⌘C ⌘X ⌘VCopy, cut, paste (the pasted area floats under the pointer until you drop it).
⌘Z ⌘⇧ZUndo, redo.
Shift+arrowsMove the whole level a tile.
' C NGrid, collision lines, tile numbers.

⌘ is Ctrl on Windows and Linux. Tiles are 64 px; 1 tile is 2.5 m. The stage you're editing is kept in this browser as you go.
