Drag a box. Drag inside it to move it; Alt-drag to move a copy. Arrow keys nudge it a tile.

A moved or pasted area floats over the stage until you click outside it, press Enter or switch tools, so you can keep moving it without wiping what's underneath.

Click to add points; the tiles follow the curve, joining the ground either side, with fill chosen so the dirt's edge carries on from tile to tile.

Drag a point or a handle to reshape (Alt-drag a handle for a corner). Right-click a point to remove it, double-click to smooth it.

Click (or drag) where the truck drops in.

Click where the finish line goes; drag to move it. With none, the stage goes on until you quit, like a stunt track.

Stage Editor: keyboard and mouse

Tools

B PaintDrag paints the chosen tile, right-drag erases. Alt-click picks up a tile; H swaps the chosen tile for its mirror.
S SelectDrag a box; drag inside to move it, Alt-drag to copy. H flips it, arrows nudge it, Del clears it, Enter drops it, Esc lets go.
D PathClick to add points to a curve; the tiles follow it. Drag points and handles; right-click removes a point.
T StartClick where the truck drops in (or Shift-click while painting).
F FinishClick where the finish line goes.

Everywhere

Pinch, ⌘/Ctrl+scrollZoom at the pointer. + − zoom, 0 fits the height.
Scroll, Space+drag, middle-dragMove around. The strip at the bottom shows the whole level: click it to jump.
⌘C ⌘X ⌘VCopy, cut, paste (the pasted area floats under the pointer until you drop it).
⌘Z ⌘⇧ZUndo, redo.
Shift+arrowsMove the whole level a tile.
G C NGrid, collision lines, tile numbers.

⌘ is Ctrl on Windows and Linux. Tiles are 64 px; 1 tile is 2.5 m. The stage you're editing is kept in this browser as you go.
